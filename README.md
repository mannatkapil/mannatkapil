## 📡 What is "agentic AI" and why does it fascinate me?

Most people think of AI as a chatbot that answers questions.

Agentic AI is different — it's AI that **takes actions**, **makes decisions**, **uses tools**, and **works toward goals** over multiple steps. It reasons. It loops. It fails and retries.

I find that endlessly interesting. I want to understand how these systems actually work — not just use them, but *build* them.
